📖 How To Play

🎮
Goal: Guide the hungry snake to eat as much food as possible and score maximum points.
🍎
Growth: Each food item awards +10 points and lengthens the snake by one segment.
⚡
Speed: As your score climbs, the snake gradually slithers faster!
💥
Hazards: Crashing into the border walls or biting your own tail results in Game Over.
🕹️
Controls:
  • Arrows / WASD: Change Direction
  • P or Space: Pause / Resume
  • R: Quick Restart
  • Mobile: Swipe on screen or tap the on-screen D-Pad
