TACTICAL MANUAL

1. The 8×8 Cyber Grid & Frequencies

PSEUDOKO takes place on an 8×8 coordinate matrix. You deploy four fundamental neural energy frequencies: Alpha (α), Beta (β), Gamma (γ), and Delta (δ).

2. Sudoku-Inspired Deduction Constraints

The board is partitioned into sixteen 2×2 Sectors. No sector may contain duplicate frequencies, and nodes of identical frequency cannot be placed directly adjacent to one another. Every placement demands pure logical deduction!

3. Harmonic Circuits & Resonance Combos

When all 4 unique frequencies (α, β, γ, δ) are united within a 2×2 sector or along a 4-node line conduit, you trigger a Harmonic Circuit Resonance! This locks the sector, grants permanent territory control, awards massive score multipliers, and returns +1 bonus move.

4. Special Strategic Cells

  • ⚡ Power Core: Grants +300 points, radiates permanent quadrant influence, and recharges +1 move when occupied.
  • 🛡 Encrypted Firewall: Shields cells from standard placements. Bypass with parity harmonic alignment or use your EMP ability.
  • 🌀 Vortex Conduit: Radiates influence diagonally across the entire grid.

5. Tactical Abilities

Use Scan (🔍) to compute mathematically optimal placement vectors, EMP (⚡) to disintegrate firewalls, and Undo (↺) to reverse unintended moves.
